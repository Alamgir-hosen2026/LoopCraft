1. Summary

  • No account, no sign-in. You can use LoopCraft without creating an account.
  • No developer server. The app does not send your songs, loops, notes, practice history or backups to any server operated by us.
  • Your content stays on your phone. Everything you add is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. LoopCraft does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Microphone is used only for the Tuner, in real time. Sound is analysed on your device and is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • Backups are made by you as a .lkb file that you keep wherever you choose.

2. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LoopCraft — Music Practice Studio (the "App", "LoopCraft") handles information when you use it. The App is developed and published by Alamgir App Studio ("we", "us", "our") for Android devices.

By installing and using LoopCraft you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

4. Information we handle and how

4.1 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, and the App does not collect or transmit to us: your name, e-mail address, phone number, address, contacts, photos, location, advertising ID, or any account credentials. There is no registration.

4.2 Information you create and store on your device

The following is created by you while using the App and is stored only on your device, in the App's private storage and local database:

  • Audio files you import (a private copy is made so the App can play them and include them in backups).
  • Song details: title, artist, duration, detected BPM and first-beat time.
  • Loops: name, notes, start and end time, speed and pitch.
  • Section markers, practice sets and the order of songs in each set.
  • Practice history: for each session, the song, date and time, duration and number of loop repeats.
  • Preferences: for example count-in on/off and beats, metronome BPM and beats per bar, last selected tuner instrument.
  • Waveform cache: a small file per song to speed up opening.
  • Subscription status: a simple flag and the time it was last verified, so the App can work briefly offline.

4.3 How the information is collected

  • From you directly: when you choose audio files with the Android file picker, type names or notes, create sets or markers, change settings, or press buttons in the App.
  • From your audio files: the App reads the file's embedded title, artist and duration, and analyses the audio on-device to draw the waveform and estimate BPM.
  • From your use of the App: while a song plays, the App measures playing time and counts loop repeats to build your practice history.
  • From the microphone: only while the Tuner screen is open and you have allowed the permission (see section 6).

4.4 How the information is used

  • To provide the features described in section 3.
  • To show your library, loops, sets, history and statistics.
  • To create backups and restore them when you ask.
  • To check that your subscription is active.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale.

4.5 Your audio files and copyright

You are responsible for having the right to use the audio files you import. The App does not upload, share or publish your audio files, and we do not review them.

6. Microphone and the Tuner

The microphone is used only by the Instrument Tuner and only while the Tuner screen is open.

  • The sound is analysed in real time on your device to find the note you are playing.
  • The sound is not recorded, not saved to storage, and not sent anywhere.
  • Listening stops when you leave the Tuner screen or put the App in the background.
  • You can deny or withdraw the permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → LoopCraft → Permissions.

7. Subscription and payments

LoopCraft requires an auto-renewing yearly subscription (currently listed at US$2.99 per year; the price shown in Google Play for your country is the one that applies).

  • Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. Your card, bank or other payment details are handled by Google and are never given to us or stored by the App.
  • The App uses the Google Play Billing Library to ask Google Play whether your subscription is active, and to show the localised price.
  • We do not receive your name or e-mail address through this process, and no developer server is involved in checking the subscription.
  • You can view, manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions, or from Tools → Manage Subscription inside the App. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policies.
  • Google's use of your information is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Backup, restore and sharing

LoopCraft does not use any cloud account or developer server for backups. Backups are created only when you ask.

  • What a backup contains: a single file with the extension .lkb (named like LoopCraft_2026-10-28.lkb). It is a ZIP-style package that contains your saved data (songs list, loops, notes, markers, sets, practice history) and the audio files you imported.
  • Where it goes: after you press Backup Data you choose either Save to phone (a location you pick) or Share, which opens the Android Share Sheet so you can send the file to an app such as Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, WhatsApp or e-mail.
  • Your responsibility: if you share the backup with another app or service, that app's own privacy policy applies to the file from then on. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. It may contain your notes and your music files.
  • Restore: when you choose a .lkb file, the App reads it on your device and replaces the current App data with the contents of the backup.
  • Exported clips: WAV files you export are saved to the location you choose in the system file picker. They are yours to keep or share.

9. Third-party services

LoopCraft uses only the following third-party components:

  • Google Play Billing Library / Google Play — to process and verify the subscription (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Open-source Android libraries (for example AndroidX, Material Components, Room database and Media3/ExoPlayer for audio playback). These run inside the App on your device and do not send your data to us.

The App does not include advertising networks, analytics services, social-media SDKs, or crash-reporting services of its own.

Please note that Google Play may separately collect and provide us with aggregated, anonymous technical information about the App (for example crash and performance statistics, install counts and device model categories) through the Google Play Console. We do not receive information that identifies you personally through this.

10. Do we share or sell data?

No. We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal information, because we do not collect it. Your library, loops, notes, practice history and audio files remain on your device unless you choose to back up, export or share them.

We may disclose information only if required by law, which is unlikely because we hold no user data.

11. Where data is stored and security

  • All App data is stored in the App's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot normally read it, and it is protected by Android's app sandbox and, on most devices, device encryption.
  • Automatic Android cloud backup of the App's data is turned off in the App. The only backup is the one you create yourself.
  • We use reasonable measures to protect the App, but no method of storage is completely secure. You are responsible for the security of your device (for example screen lock) and of any backup files you create.

12. Retention and deletion

  • Inside the App: you can delete songs (which also removes their loops, markers, set entries, history and the stored audio copy), delete loops, markers and sets at any time.
  • Everything at once: uninstalling the App, or choosing Android Settings → Apps → LoopCraft → Storage → Clear data, removes all App data from your device.
  • Backup files and exported clips you saved or shared are outside the App; delete them yourself from where you stored them.
  • Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted and you have no backup.

LoopCraft is a general music-practice tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. A parent or guardian should manage the Google Play purchase for a young user. 14. Your rights and choices

Because your data is stored only on your own device, you are in control of it:

  • Access and portability: open the App to see your data, or create a .lkb backup to hold a complete copy.
  • Correction: rename songs and loops, and edit notes inside the App.
  • Deletion: delete items in the App or clear/uninstall the App (section 12).
  • Permissions: grant or revoke the microphone permission any time in Android Settings.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel in Google Play.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have additional legal rights regarding personal data. As we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete, but you may contact us at any time with a request or question.
